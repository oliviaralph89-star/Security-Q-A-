### Q1: The Multi-Layered Guard
A security guard checks IDs at a front gate. In which Category and Type does this control fall?

Answer: Category: Physical (limits access to a facility). Type: Preventive (blocks unauthorized people from passing).

### Q2: The "After-the-Fact" Fix
You discover a server has been infected with ransomware and immediately restore it from a known-good backup. What Control Type did you just use?

Answer: Corrective. It is used after an event to fix the issue and restore the environment to its original state.

### Q3: The CIA Missing Link
An attacker performs a Denial of Service (DoS) attack on your website. The data is still encrypted and unchanged, but no customers can reach it. Which leg of the CIA Triad is broken?

Answer: Availability. The systems and data are not accessible when needed by authorized users.

### Q4 Proof of Action
A user claims they never sent a specific malicious email from their account, but your system shows a Digital Signature that only their private key could have created. What concept is this?

Answer: Non-Repudiation. It provides proof of origin and integrity so a party cannot deny their actions.
